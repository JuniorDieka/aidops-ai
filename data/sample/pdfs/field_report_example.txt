HUMANITARIAN FIELD REPORT
Date: January 15, 2026
Location: Northern Region, Humanitarian Zone Alpha
Report ID: FR-2026-001

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This field report documents the assessment of humanitarian needs in the Northern Region following recent flooding events. The assessment team identified critical gaps in water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) services affecting approximately 15,000 displaced persons.

SITUATION OVERVIEW
Heavy rainfall over the past two weeks has resulted in widespread flooding across the Northern Region. Approximately 3,500 families (15,000 individuals) have been displaced from their homes and are currently sheltering in temporary camps and host communities.

NEEDS ASSESSMENT

Water and Sanitation:
- Only 2 functional water points serving the entire displaced population
- Current water availability: 5 liters per person per day (below Sphere minimum standard of 15 liters)
- No functional latrines in 60% of temporary shelters
- High risk of waterborne disease outbreak

Health Services:
- Mobile health clinic operational 3 days per week
- Limited medical supplies, particularly oral rehydration salts and antibiotics
- 45 cases of acute watery diarrhea reported in the past week
- Vaccination coverage estimated at 40% for children under 5

Food Security:
- Food distribution conducted weekly by local NGO partners
- Current rations provide approximately 1,800 kcal per person per day (below 2,100 kcal standard)
- No specialized nutrition products available for malnourished children

Shelter:
- 70% of displaced families living in makeshift shelters (plastic sheeting, branches)
- Average living space: 2.5 square meters per person (below 3.5 sqm standard)
- Limited protection from elements

RECOMMENDATIONS

Immediate Actions (0-2 weeks):
1. Install additional water points to achieve minimum 15 liters per person per day
2. Construct emergency latrines (target: 1 latrine per 20 people)
3. Distribute hygiene kits to all displaced families
4. Increase mobile health clinic frequency to daily operations
5. Procure and distribute emergency shelter materials

Short-term Actions (2-8 weeks):
1. Establish water treatment and distribution system
2. Implement community-led total sanitation approach
3. Set up nutrition screening and treatment program
4. Conduct vaccination campaign targeting children under 5
5. Provide livelihood support to affected families

COORDINATION
This response is being coordinated with:
- Ministry of Health
- National Disaster Management Authority
- UN OCHA
- Local NGO partners (Water for Life, Health Access International)

BUDGET ESTIMATE
Estimated funding requirement for 3-month emergency response: USD 450,000

Breakdown:
- WASH: $180,000
- Health: $120,000
- Food Security: $90,000
- Shelter: $60,000
