GLOBAL HUMANITARIAN FUND
GRANT APPLICATION GUIDELINES 2026

OVERVIEW
The Global Humanitarian Fund (GHF) provides financial support to organizations implementing humanitarian assistance programs in crisis-affected areas. This document outlines the application process, eligibility criteria, and reporting requirements.

FUNDING PRIORITIES
The GHF prioritizes projects that:
1. Address immediate life-saving needs
2. Strengthen community resilience
3. Promote gender equality and social inclusion
4. Demonstrate cost-effectiveness and sustainability
5. Align with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

ELIGIBLE SECTORS
- Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH)
- Health and Nutrition
- Food Security and Livelihoods
- Shelter and Non-Food Items
- Protection and Psychosocial Support
- Education in Emergencies

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Organizational Requirements:
- Registered non-profit organization with minimum 2 years operational history
- Demonstrated experience in humanitarian response
- Financial management systems meeting international standards
- Safeguarding and accountability policies in place
- Previous GHF grantees in good standing

Geographic Focus:
- Projects must be implemented in GHF-designated crisis zones
- Priority given to underserved and hard-to-reach populations
- Multi-country projects considered on case-by-case basis

FUNDING PARAMETERS
- Grant Size: USD 50,000 - USD 500,000
- Project Duration: 6-18 months
- Overhead Costs: Maximum 7% of total budget
- Cost-sharing: 10% contribution from applicant organization preferred

APPLICATION PROCESS

Phase 1: Concept Note (Due: March 1, 2026)
Submit 3-page concept note including:
- Problem statement and needs assessment
- Proposed intervention and target population
- Expected outcomes and impact
- Preliminary budget

Phase 2: Full Proposal (By invitation only)
Selected applicants will be invited to submit full proposals including:
- Detailed project description and logical framework
- Comprehensive budget with narrative justification
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Monitoring and evaluation plan
- Organizational capacity statement

PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

Needs Assessment:
- Evidence-based analysis of humanitarian needs
- Consultation with affected communities
- Gender and age-disaggregated data
- Coordination with other actors

Project Design:
- Clear objectives aligned with Sphere Standards
- Theory of change demonstrating impact pathway
- Inclusion of cross-cutting themes (gender, protection, environment)
- Exit strategy and sustainability plan

Budget:
- Detailed line-item budget
- Unit costs and quantities clearly specified
- Alignment with sector benchmarks
- Separate budget for monitoring and evaluation (minimum 5%)

SELECTION CRITERIA
Proposals will be evaluated based on:
1. Relevance and strategic fit (25%)
2. Technical quality and feasibility (25%)
3. Cost-effectiveness (20%)
4. Organizational capacity (15%)
5. Sustainability and impact (15%)

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Financial Reports:
- Quarterly financial reports using GHF template
- Final financial report within 60 days of project completion
- External audit required for grants exceeding USD 200,000

Narrative Reports:
- Quarterly progress reports (maximum 10 pages)
- Final narrative report (maximum 20 pages)
- Case studies and success stories
- Lessons learned and recommendations

Monitoring and Evaluation:
- Baseline survey within first month
- Mid-term evaluation for projects exceeding 12 months
- End-line survey and impact assessment
- Beneficiary feedback mechanism

COMPLIANCE AND SAFEGUARDING
All grantees must:
- Adhere to GHF Code of Conduct
- Implement safeguarding policies protecting beneficiaries and staff
- Report any incidents of fraud, corruption, or abuse
- Maintain proper documentation for 7 years post-project
- Allow GHF monitoring visits and audits
